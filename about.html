Personal Profile

The formal pages of this site cover my research, projects, code, and professional record. This page is the less formal counterpart: the places, habits, and interests that shape the person behind the work.

Outside research, I spend as much time as I can hiking, traveling, reading, and building small technical projects. I am especially drawn to mountains, national parks, unfamiliar places, and books that change how I understand a problem—or simply build a world worth getting lost in.

Military Service

U.S. Army

My background includes service in U.S. Army infantry roles, with experience in airborne operations, communications, small-unit leadership, and arctic and mountain environments. Military service has shaped how I approach difficult problems: value clear communication, work within real constraints, and prefer analysis that remains useful when conditions become uncertain and imperfect.
