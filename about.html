Military Service

U.S. Army

Before beginning graduate study, I served in the U.S. Army as a paratrooper and squad leader in an airborne infantry unit. I was responsible for leading soldiers, maintaining accountability and readiness, and communicating clearly in environments where incomplete information, physical stress, and changing conditions were routine.

Much of that experience took place in Alaska and included Arctic and extreme-cold-weather training and operations. Military service remains an important part of how I approach technical and analytical work: define the problem clearly, distinguish what is known from what is assumed, communicate decisions in plain language, and build solutions that remain useful outside ideal conditions. The CV contains the formal chronology; here, the important point is that the Army was a formative part of my professional and personal development.
