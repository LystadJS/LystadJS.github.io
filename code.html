R Package

driftmapR

A moving map does not necessarily mean a changing system.

I am developing driftmapR to compare repeated low-dimensional representations of the same observations. The package aligns corresponding points, measures residual movement, and supports repeated-fit comparisons so differences between maps can be examined rather than assumed to be meaningful.

What remains
after alignment?

Separate runs can rotate, reflect, or translate a map while preserving its underlying relationships. Alignment puts matched observations in a common frame, making the differences that remain easier to measure and inspect.

Residual movement still needs explanation. Differences in sample composition, measurement, or algorithmic stability may resemble structural change. Resampling can probe that sensitivity, but the project's inferential reliability remains under validation.
