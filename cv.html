Overview

I am currently as a M.S. candidate in Applied Statistics and Computational Social Science at New York University, preparing to pursue a PhD in Computational Statistics

My background sits at the intersection of computational statistics, open-source intelligence, military leadership, and social science research. Before beginning graduate studies, I served in the U.S. Army as a paratrooper and squad leader in an airborne infantry unit, where I was responsible for the leading soldiers in high-pressure environments in Arctic and extreme-cold-weather conditions. My military experience taught me to value disciplined analysis, clear communication, and decisions that survive contact with reality.
