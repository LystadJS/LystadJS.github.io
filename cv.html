John S. Lystad

Computational statistics for consequential decisions.

jl17842@nyu.edu lystadjs@gmail.com github.com/LystadJS
Profile

Researcher, statistician, and computer scientist working at the intersection of applied computational statistics and social science, with an emphasis on decision-making under uncertainty, imperfect real-world data conditions, and high-consequence policy environments.

Primary substantive interests include humanitarian crises, counterterrorism, deradicalization, human security, and emerging technology governance.
