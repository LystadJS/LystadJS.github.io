Scholarly Work & Publications

UNPUBLISHED WORK-IN-PROGRESS

“ Apocalyptic and Eschatological Speech as a Predictor of Violence by The Islamic State and its Associated Movement ”

  • Can references to an imminent apocalypse in Islamic State propaganda help forecast violence beyond what recent attacks and publication volume already tell us? I piloted text extraction on 12 Arabic issues of al-Naba to assess whether the timing and frequency of such references could be measured reliably. The pilot yielded 14 verified cover passages and showed that full-page extraction still needs correction before a forecasting model can be tested.

Lystad, J. S. (In Progress). “Apocalyptic and Eschatological Speech as a Predictor of Violence by the Islamic State and its Associated Movement.”

In Progress

UNPUBLISHED WORK-IN-PROGRESS

“ The Caliphate’s Target Map: Ethnosectarian Composition and Insurgent Violence in Iraq ”

  • National attack totals obscure whether the Islamic State selected different targets in Iraqi districts with different ethnosectarian populations. I combined Global Terrorism Database records, district demographics, and LandScan population estimates to compare attack frequency and targeting across phases of the insurgency. The resulting district-month dataset distinguishes attacks on civilians, security forces, and religious targets within each district.

Lystad, J. S. (In Progress). “The Caliphate’s Target Map: Ethnosectarian Composition and Insurgent Violence in Iraq.”

In Progress

UNPUBLISHED WORK-IN-PROGRESS

“ Estimating Lethality Under Missing Casualty Data: Suicide Attacks in Islamic State-Linked Violence ”

  • Missing fatality counts complicate estimates of how much more lethal suicide attacks are than other Islamic State-linked attacks. I compared complete-case analysis, weighting, and single and multiple imputation across 16,227 incidents in 46 countries from 1994 to 2021 to test how sensitive that comparison was to missing data. Across methods, suicide attacks remained associated with higher fatalities after adjustment.

Lystad, J. S. (In Progress). “Estimating Lethality Under Missing Casualty Data: Suicide Attacks in Islamic State-Linked Terrorism.”

2022

HONORS THESIS

“ Vanguards of Terror: The Strategic and Tactical Orientation and Cohesion of the Islamic State Insurgency: 1999 – 2019 ”

  • A shared Islamic State affiliation leaves open how closely regional groups followed central leadership in their choice of tactics and targets. I developed nine indicators of organizational ties and compared them with Global Terrorism Database attack records to assess cohesion across affiliates. The comparison showed that affiliates differed in both the tactics they used and the targets they selected.

Lystad, J. S. (2022). Vanguards of Terror: The Strategic and Tactical Orientation and Cohesion of the Islamic State Insurgency: 1999 – 2019. [Honors thesis, Florida State University]. FSU DigiNole Repository. https://repository.lib.fsu.edu/islandora/object/fsu:802361.

2020

PEER-REVIEWED

“ The Effects of Climate Change on the Development of Terrorist Networks in the Middle East and North Africa ”

  • How might climate change affect recruitment by the Islamic State and al-Qaeda in the Middle East and North Africa? I compared NASA projections of temperature, precipitation, and evaporation with historical recruitment records to examine how environmental pressure might shape recruitment in selected countries. The study identified resource scarcity and governments’ capacity to respond as possible links between environmental stress and recruitment.

Lystad, J. S. (2020). “The Effects of Climate Change on the Development of Terrorist Networks in the Middle East and North Africa.” The Owl — Florida State University’s Undergraduate Research Journal, 10(1), 61–80. https://journals.flvc.org/owl/article/view/119038/129613.

WHITE PAPER · U.S. MISSION TO THE UNITED NATIONS

“ Detecting Emerging Threats to UN Peacekeepers from Latent Structure Discovery ”

  • Individual attacks on UN peacekeepers offer little indication of whether a mission faces a recurring threat or how that threat relates to local violence. I combined open-source attack and mission records and used unsupervised learning to look for recurring patterns across incidents. The combined records provide a basis for testing whether those patterns signal emerging threats or coincide with reductions in violence.

Lystad, J. S. (In Progress). Detecting Emerging Threats to UN Peacekeepers from Latent Structure Discovery [White Paper]. U.S. Mission to the United Nations.

In Progress

WHITE PAPER · U.S. MISSION TO THE UNITED NATIONS

“ Discovering Latent Data Patterns in UN General Assembly & Security Council Voting Behavior: 2000 - 2026 ”

  • Agreement on a single UN vote reveals little about whether a diplomatic alignment will persist. I used dimension reduction and clustering on General Assembly and Security Council votes from 2000 to 2026 to identify blocs and track changes in their membership. Those historical voting patterns now serve as inputs for testing how well earlier alignments predict subsequent votes.

Lystad, J. S. (In Progress). Discovering Latent Data Patterns in UN General Assembly and Security Council Voting Behavior: 2000 – 2026. [White Paper]. U.S. Mission to the United Nations.

TECHNICAL HANDBOOK · U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

“ The 11th Airborne Division Operator’s Guide to Radio Communication Operations in the Arctic ”

  • Extreme cold makes tactical radios harder to operate and maintain, and relevant guidance was dispersed across equipment specifications, experiments, and operators’ experience. I brought those sources together in a handbook for the 11th Airborne Division to give radio operators procedures suited to Arctic conditions. The handbook established common standards for operating and maintaining tactical radios in the field.

Lystad, J. S. (2025). The 11th Airborne Division Operator’s Guide to Rdio Communication Operations in the Arctic. U.S. Department of Defense.

TECHNICAL REPORT · U.S. DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE

“ Assessing the Effects of Extreme Cold Weather on Communications Hardware Battery Life: BB-2590/U, BB-5590/U, RF-2525/U, and BT-70716BE ”

  • Arctic conditions make it difficult for units to estimate how long their communications batteries will last in the field. I compared the endurance of four military battery models under different cold-weather conditions to assess how operating conditions constrain radio use. The report gives leaders and operators model-specific comparisons they can use when planning battery supplies.

Lystad, J. S. (2024). Assessing the Effects of Extreme Cold Weather on Communications Hardware Battery Life: BB-2590/U, BB-5590/U, RF-2525/U, and BT-70716BE. U.S. Department of Defense.

Technical Development & Software

In Progress

R PACKAGE

driftmapR

  • When high-dimensional data are mapped into fewer dimensions at successive time points, coordinates can shift even if the underlying relationships remain stable. I am developing driftmapR to compare repeated representations and quantify uncertainty about which changes reflect shifts in data structure. The package is designed to help researchers judge which apparent changes warrant interpretation in a longitudinal analysis.
In Progress

R · PYTHON · JAVASCRIPT

‘transcripts.un.org’ Latent Data Discovery Software

  • Researchers comparing UN transcripts need to know whether recurring themes are stable and which passages support them. I am developing software that organizes transcripts and combines clustering, dimension reduction, and source-linked review to investigate patterns across meetings. Researchers can trace summaries back to their source documents and use stability diagnostics to assess how consistently themes appear.
