      02 / Application Umbrella

      Human Security.

      My applied research is organized around a common concern: understanding risks that can produce substantial consequences for individuals, institutions, and societies.

      Human Security therefore sits above the application taxonomy rather than inside the network itself. The applied program is organized into three intersecting domains.

      01 / Application Domain

      Catastrophic &
      Emerging Risks

      Emerging technologies, systemic threats, low-frequency/high-impact events, and the institutions that respond to them. Technology and AI governance sit within this domain.

      AI governance Technology diffusion Systemic risk Early warning

      02 / Application Domain

      Counterterrorism &
      Counterextremism

      Terrorist and extremist networks, organizational behavior, adaptation, mobilization, violence, and intervention-relevant structure.

      Networks Diffusion Temporal change Risk analysis

      03 / Application Domain

      Humanitarian
      Crisis Response

      Civilian harm, disasters, displacement, food insecurity, humanitarian access, and quantitative support for crisis anticipation and response.

      Civilian harm Disasters Forecasting Response

      04 / Methods & Software

      Four methodological areas.

      The categories describe how I approach problems; application domains describe where those methods are used.

      Method 01

      Statistical Learning & Unsupervised Learning

      Clustering, dimensionality reduction, mixture models, latent structure, and anomaly detection.

      Method 02

      Statistical Forecasting & Risk Analysis

      Probabilistic forecasting, early warning, event-history models, Bayesian updating, and uncertainty.

      Method 03

      Network & Diffusion Analysis

      Communities, centrality, diffusion, influence, relational structure, and temporal networks.

      Method 04

      Longitudinal & Dynamic Modeling

      Multilevel, panel, trajectory, repeated-measures, and evolving-system models.
