I develop and employ statistical and computational methods for problems where the data is imperfect and errors carry real consequences. My research emphasizes unsupervised learning methods, uncovering latent structure, missing and messy data, dimension reduction and clustering, and anticipatory forecasting, with applications to human security, emerging threats, humanitarian crises, political violence, and climate risk. Across these domains, my approach is to recover defensible, nonrandom structure from imperfect data, quantify uncertainty, and derive inferences that are useful for forecasting emerging danger and intervening before the risk intensifies.

Current work: open-source AI and autonomous weapon non-proliferation.

The question I want to answer:
