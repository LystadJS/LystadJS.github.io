Published May 2026

The Caliphate's Target Map: Ethnosectarian Composition and Insurgent Violence in Iraq

Examined whether Islamic State attack patterns in Iraq aligned with its sectarian ideology by linking insurgent violence to district-level ethnosectarian composition, population-weighted geography, and target-type modeling. Identified concentrated attack activity in Sunni and religiously fractionalized areas, alongside elevated civilian targeting in non-Sunni districts.

Estimating Lethality Under Missing Casualty Data: Suicide Attacks in Islamic State-Linked Terrorism

Analyzed missing casualty data and perpetrator-attribution bias in terrorism event datasets, demonstrating how incomplete open-source records can alter statistical conclusions about attack lethality and terrorist tactical behavior.

Vanguards of Terror: The Strategic and Tactical Orientation and Cohesion of the Islamic State Insurgency: 1999-2019

Compared the tactical and strategic cohesion of wilayat within the Islamic State and its Associated Movement, investigating the inner workings of the Islamic State and demonstrating that the operational behavior of wilayat within the Islamic State are not uniform.

The Effects of Climate Change on the Development of Terrorist Networks in the Middle East and North Africa

Analyzed how projected changes in temperature, precipitation, evaporation, and heat stress could intensify resource scarcity, weaken governance, and create conditions exploitable by terrorist organizations in the Middle East. Used NASA Global Climate Model scenario comparisons to connect environmental change with recruitment dynamics and regional instability.
