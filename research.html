02 / Questions

Questions I want to answer

Use this section as the site matures to make your research identity legible before you have a long publication list.

Inference

What survives bad data?

How should inference change when data are missing, biased, heterogeneous, or produced for operational rather than research purposes?

Governance

How does openness change proliferation?

What changes when strategically relevant technical capability diffuses through open-source models, code, and tooling?

Human systems

How can risk be measured responsibly?

What can statistical models contribute when the objects of analysis are people, institutions, and high-consequence environments?

Translation

When does evidence change a decision?

How should uncertainty be communicated when analysis must move from model output to policy or practice?
