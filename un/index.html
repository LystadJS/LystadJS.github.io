01 / Current project

UN Transcript Intelligence

The workspace turns UN proceedings into a searchable, source-linked record. Choose dates, meetings, and topics, then examine recurring language, compare groups of passages, and open the source behind each pattern. Coverage checks identify gaps in the available record.
