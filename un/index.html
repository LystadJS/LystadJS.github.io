01CURRENT PROJECTTRANSCRIPT INTELLIGENCE

UN Transcript Intelligence

A public workflow for collecting UN transcripts by date and meeting scope, searching a topic or collecting the full available record, and moving from descriptive patterns back to the exact source passages.

The browser workspace combines coverage checks, source-linked review, TF-IDF, dimensional representations, clustering, overlapping NMF components, stability diagnostics, and downloadable reports. The goal is not to replace close reading; it is to make a large transcript record easier to inspect systematically.

02ONGOINGPEACEKEEPING OPTIMIZATION

Optimizing UN Peacekeeping Efforts

An ongoing project developing a systematic way to compare peacekeeping conditions, operational constraints, and resource trade-offs so that alternative mission configurations can be evaluated more clearly.

The public-facing design will emphasize the decision problem: different missions face different mandates, risks, logistical constraints, and resource limits. The analytical objective is to make those trade-offs explicit and testable without presenting a model as a substitute for political or operational judgment.

03APPLIED PROJECTHUMANITARIAN RESPONSE

Himalayan Flood Response

An applied analysis of humanitarian access and response effectiveness along the Nepalese-Chinese border, focused on how difficult operating conditions shape the ability to reach affected communities.

The project treats access as a system rather than a single distance: terrain, crossing points, transport corridors, and response pathways can create very different practical levels of reach. The aim is to communicate those constraints clearly enough to support operational comparison and prioritization.

04OPEN-SOURCE LAYERAI GOVERNANCE

AI Governance Diffusion & Non-Proliferation

An open-source research program examining AI governance, non-proliferation, and the multilateral process terrain through which policy ideas move, change, and potentially become institutionalized.

The public reproducibility layer converts structured process-evidence profiles into a mixed-data distance, applies a correction for non-Euclidean structure, and uses principal coordinates analysis to map institutional similarity. The broader project asks how governance provisions diffuse across venues while keeping similarity distinct from influence or political alignment.
