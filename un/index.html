02 / Ongoing

Streamlining UN Peace Operations

This project asks where overlapping responsibilities create duplicated work and avoidable costs across UN peace operations. It compares the mandates, activities, and budgets of the Department of Peace Operations (DPO), the Department of Political and Peacebuilding Affairs (DPPA), and related bodies.

Shared responsibilities can strengthen coordination or duplicate effort. The aim is to distinguish the two, identify work that could be consolidated, and assess whether resources can be redirected without weakening peacekeeping operations.
